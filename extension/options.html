Your inbox, with care.

Your job profile and matching emails (sender, subject, and up to 16,000 body characters) are sent to NVIDIA to identify relevant jobs, direct messages, and draft replies. Email attachments are not sent to AI. Drafts are saved in Gmail and require your review before sending.

Choosing Generate with AI sends only the event form’s title, dates, location, and rough notes to NVIDIA. Selecting a ticket PDF automatically uploads it to your backend and sends its extracted text, including any passenger and booking details, to NVIDIA. The PDF is processed temporarily and is not stored or attached to Calendar. Extracted details automatically fill the event form and are saved to Google Calendar only when you choose Save event.

Google tokens, your job profile, email text, drafts, and calendar data are encrypted in the backend data folder. Disconnect clears that data and revokes Google access; drafts already saved in Gmail remain there. The pairing token is stored in this Chrome profile.
